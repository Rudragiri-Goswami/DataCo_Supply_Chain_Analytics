# Supply Chain Analytics

### Delivery Performance & Late Delivery Prediction

## Overview

An end-to-end supply chain analytics project focused on understanding delivery performance and predicting late deliveries.

## Problem

Late deliveries can impact customer satisfaction and operational efficiency. The objective is to identify patterns associated with delivery delays and factors linked to higher late-delivery risk.

## Approach

- Cleaned and prepared supply chain data
- Performed exploratory and delivery performance analysis
- Identified patterns across shipping, regional, customer, and time dimensions
- Built classification models to predict late-delivery risk

## Tech Stack

**Python · Pandas · Scikit-learn · Matplotlib · Seaborn · Jupyter Notebook**

## Model

Tested Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest.

**Decision Tree** achieved the best F1 Score of **0.763**.

## Outcome

Identified key delivery patterns and developed a predictive model for late-delivery risk.
